# Skill: SEO and AdSense-Safe Monetization

Use for pages, content, indexing, and ads.

Rules:

- unique useful page intent;
- working tool before excessive copy;
- metadata/canonical/breadcrumbs;
- visible, accurate FAQ only;
- no fake ratings or claims;
- no thin planned pages indexed;
- ads clearly distinguished;
- no ads beside upload/process/download/navigation controls;
- no auto-refresh, click encouragement, arrows, or misleading headings;
- conservative formats first;
- ads disabled by default and fail closed.
